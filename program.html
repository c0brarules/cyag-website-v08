★ Our Program

Designed to excite youth ages 12–18 through immersive aerospace, aviation, STEM, robotics, and simulator experiences within a structured, safe, learning environment.

What We Deliver

Programs for Every Stage

We provide hands-on instruction using flight simulators, STEM tools and aerospace design learning systems through a dedicated, volunteer crew of experienced aviators and aerospace industry professionals.

  • ✈️

    Our program includes:

    • Mentorship, coaching, and career guidance
    • Leadership development through team participation
    • Collaboration and optional participation with local Cadet programs
    • Scholarship support
