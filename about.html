★ Our Story

Since 2005, we’ve been creating life-changing experiences for youth through innovative, hands on experiential aerospace training.

    • Team centred, mentorship driven
    • Safe, inclusive, inspiring learning environment
    • Real experiences, Real skills, Real opportunities
    • Preparing youth for success in an aerospace future

We encourage all volunteers and participants to uphold our values.

    • Respect
    • Loyalty
    • Accountability
    • Reliability
    • Dedication

To inspire and empower youth to explore and pursue aerospace career opportunities.

Our Vision

Milestones in Flight

To build a graduated aerospace training program that provides youth with accessible, skill-based, experiential learning—developing their knowledge, confidence, leadership, and technical abilities as they explore aviation, aerospace, and STEM career pathways.
